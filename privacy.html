Your privacy

Rwendo is designed to keep each user's placement records separate and protected.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information used by the app

When you choose to sign in with Google, the app uses Firebase Authentication to identify your account.

Google may provide basic account information such as your name, email address, profile picture and a unique account identifier.

The app also stores information you enter or create while using it, including:

  • Placement shift dates
  • Start and finish times
  • Break durations
  • Calculated placement hours
  • Your placement-hours target
  • Shift notes you write
  • Supervision sessions, including topics, reflections and actions
  • Supervisor notes on shifts, supervision sessions and weeks, with the supervisor's name and the date
  • Supervisor sign off details: the supervisor's name, the date and the hours approved
  • A one-way hash of the supervisor PIN, and a random salt used to make it
  • Placement details such as your name, programme, organisation and supervisor
  • App settings

Everything in that list is entered by you. The app does not collect location, contacts, analytics or advertising identifiers, and it has no third-party trackers.

2. How your information is used

Your information is used only to provide the functions of Rwendo, including:

  • Authenticating your account
  • Saving and displaying your placement shifts
  • Calculating totals and progress
  • Recording supervisor notes and sign off against the right shift, session or week
  • Checking the supervisor PIN when a supervisor action is requested
  • Synchronising your data between devices
  • Restoring your records when you sign in again

3. Cloud storage

When you are signed in, placement records and selected settings are stored using Google Firebase Firestore.

Firebase Authentication and Firestore Security Rules are used to restrict normal app access so that a signed-in user can access the record associated with their own Firebase user account.

Firebase and Google may process data as technology service providers necessary to operate authentication and cloud storage.

4. Local and offline storage

Rwendo also stores a copy of app data in your browser's local storage so that the tracker can continue to work when you are offline.

Data stored locally may remain on that device until the browser's site data is cleared or removed.

Rwendo keeps its own storage area, under its own key prefix, and never reads or writes the data of any other app on the same web address.

Each account gets its own separate local store on the device, and there is a further separate store for use before anyone signs in. On a shared or library computer this means one person's records are never shown to, mixed with, or uploaded to the account of the next person who signs in. Signing out hides your records until you sign back in.

If you have used the app without signing in and then sign in for the first time on that device, the app asks before it adds those records to your account. It never moves them without you choosing to.

5. The supervisor PIN

The supervisor PIN is never stored. When it is first set, Rwendo generates a random salt, combines it with the PIN, and keeps only the resulting SHA-256 hash. Checking a PIN means hashing what was typed and comparing the two hashes.

Because the PIN itself is never written down anywhere, it cannot be read out of the app, out of a backup file, or out of the cloud record — and it cannot be recovered if it is forgotten. A forgotten PIN is replaced by deleting the app's data and setting a new one.

The PIN protects a record from casual tampering on a shared device. It is not a cryptographic lock on the data itself: anyone with access to the browser's storage can still read the shifts and notes, exactly as they could read a paper timesheet left on a desk.

6. Data sharing

Rwendo does not sell your placement records or use them for advertising.

Information is processed by services required to operate the app, particularly Google Sign-In and Firebase.

7. Exports and backups

If you use CSV export, Print / PDF, or Backup / Restore, files may be created or downloaded onto your own device.

You are responsible for protecting any copies that you download, print, email or share.

8. Managing your placement records

You can edit and delete individual placement shifts from within the app.

You can also clear your saved placement history using the app's controls. When you are signed in and online, changes are synchronised with your Firebase record.

Settings includes a "Delete everything" option that erases your shifts, supervision log, supervisor notes, sign off records, the supervisor PIN hash and your placement details from this device and from your cloud record. Download a backup first if you may want the data later — deletion cannot be undone.

9. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect app data, including HTTPS, Google authentication and Firestore Security Rules.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, so users should also keep their Google account and devices secure.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when features, storage methods or services used by Rwendo change.

The latest version will be published with the application.
